Hi, I'm Dipesh. I'm a PhD student at the National University of Singapore, in the VERSE Lab, advised by Ilya Sergey.

I'm interested in programming languages, formal verification and systems programming. These days I'm working on getting verified, efficient systems code out of Lean, and maintaining and improving Velvet, a multi-modal verifier in Lean.

Before my PhD, I was a backend software engineer at Uber. Before that, I interned at IIT Madras (verifying an OCaml garbage collector in F*), Tarides (parallel incremental computation library in OCaml) and CDAC Bangalore (GCC optimizations).

Reach out to me at dipesh.kaphle111@gmail.com or dipesh@u.nus.edu. My CV is here.
